# OIA-1 v0.2.1 maintenance report

**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** deterministic policy canonicalization and release provenance only  
**Track A audit outcome rerun:** no

## Finding

Independent verification found that v0.2.0 could serialize either of two exact depth-3 adaptive identity policies depending on `PYTHONHASHSEED`. The archived policy is valid and exactly optimal. The alternate policy is also valid and exactly optimal. Root action, reachable-belief count, minimum depth, decision-depth result, black-box replay validity, and the already opened `cp437` `outside_model` outcome are unchanged.

The defect was in canonical selection among equal-depth actions. Reachable beliefs were iterated as a set, and a choice was replaced only when a strictly smaller numeric rank was found. If a lexicographically preferable equal-rank action became available in a later attractor pass, it did not replace the earlier choice.

## Narrow correction

`src/ontology_auditor/synthesis.py` now:

1. iterates the reachable belief graph in canonical sorted order;
2. computes the least terminating rank without storing discovery-time action choices; and
3. after rank convergence, chooses the lexicographically smallest action among all actions that attain the final minimum rank.

This makes canonical policy serialization independent of hash-table order while preserving the exact dynamic-programming result.
