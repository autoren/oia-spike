# Track A 005a next revision gate

## Why another slice is warranted

Track A 005a now contains a complete public archival prospective cycle:

1. fresh executable hypotheses were generated from P0 only;
2. two candidates passed exact replay, finite behavioral divergence, and the
   complete determinism matrix;
3. unchanged OIA-1 v0.2.1 selected exact minimum separator `ACTION6` before
   post-P0 outcome access;
4. five independent public recordings agreed on the resulting settled
   observation; and
5. that observation matched neither candidate.

The post-outcome diagnostic rules out a merely cosmetic exactness failure. The
no-change persistence baseline missed 2 pixels, while `C0006` and `C0008` missed
11 and 6; neither predicted either changed location. The current frontier is
therefore a useful falsified object, not a frontier to relax or rewrite.

## Research question

Can an explicitly outcome-conditioned revision procedure use only P0 and the
opened `ACTION6` transition to construct a new fixed executable frontier that:

- calibrates to the observed first transition;
- remains behaviorally plural at the resulting P1 state;
- lets unchanged OIA select a next action before step-2 outcome access; and
- makes a correct or informative outside-frontier prediction on an independently
  recorded matching step-2 transition?

This tests revision after intervention. It does not test de novo P0-only
formation again, and calibration on the already opened first outcome is not a
success endpoint.

## Evidence boundary

Authorized revision inputs should be limited to:

- canonical P0 raster and action alphabet;
- `ACTION6` as the selected first action;
- the four opened intermediate text rasters in exact order;
- the opened settled text raster and normalized `RUNNING` status;
- the immutable failure and diagnostic summaries; and
- exact model/runtime provenance already used in candidate generation.

Do not include the game identifier, archive path, run-family labels, candidate
source, step-2 metadata, step-2 frames, later traces, scores, reports, or any
private/sealed material in revision-generation prompts.

Step 2 and later remain closed. Header names may be inventoried, but no step-2
payload should be read until a revised frontier and its OIA-selected next action
are merged.

## Proposed generation gate

Use the same pinned local `mlx-community/Qwen3.8-27B-4bit` revision and offline
MLX runtime. Freeze all prompts, stances, ordering, decode settings, caps, and
acceptance rules before inference.

Generate eight revision hypotheses using distinct stances:

1. persistence with boundary-normalization dynamics;
2. transient target/impact animation with settled restoration;
3. projectile or ray interaction along the visible diagonal;
4. camera, floor, or viewport-boundary update;
5. discrete target-state machine;
6. wraparound or clipped-object motion;
7. action-conditioned latent effect with minimal visible change; and
8. a skeptical minimal countermodel.

The source contract should remain deterministic and self-contained. It must
reconstruct P0, reproduce the settled P1 observation after `ACTION6`, and then
support both actions from that revised state for at least three more steps.

### Engineering correction permitted

Six of eight original generations were rejected only because the exact custom
delimiter was absent. That condition was too strict as an engineering parser,
not as a scientific test. The revision gate may prospectively accept exactly one
unambiguous source representation:

- the original literal delimiters;
- one Python Markdown fence with no surrounding prose; or
- raw parseable Python with no prose.

The choice must be resolved by a fixed ordered parser, before AST inspection,
with zero model repair calls. Syntax, interface, import, file/service, and
isolation constraints should not be relaxed.

## Calibration and admission

Calibration requires exact P0 replay and exact settled P1 replay after
`ACTION6`. It should be reported separately from prediction and must not count as
evidence of generalization.

For each calibrated candidate:

- begin from its internally reached P1 state;
- execute all 14 words of lengths one through three over `ACTION6` and
  `ACTION7` in fresh isolated processes;
- retain the first candidate in frozen order for each observable trace
  signature;
- promote every provisional unique candidate through two repetitions under
  `PYTHONHASHSEED` 0, 1, 5, and 10; and
- require at least two replay-consistent, deterministic, behaviorally
  non-equivalent candidates. Two remains sufficient.

Primary observations should remain exact settled-frame SHA-256 plus normalized
status. Preserve descriptive pixel and P0-relative delta metrics, but do not
introduce a tolerance-based admission rule after seeing outcomes. Include the
P1-persistence predictor as a required baseline in later evaluation.

## OIA and step-2 ordering

Mechanically compile the revised traces into the same complete bounded
prefix-tree adapter. Run unchanged accepted OIA-1 v0.2.1 under hash seeds 0, 1,
5, and 10; require byte-identical reports, exact cap fields, serialized-policy
verification, and an optimality-certified minimum separator.

Merge the revised candidate frontier, adapter evidence, and selected next action
before reading any step-2 payload.

Only then may a separately frozen archival lookup:

1. read step-2 metadata for all five run roots;
2. retain every run whose recorded step-2 action exactly matches the selected
   action;
3. read only matching step-2 transition frames; and
4. compare the settled observation with every revised prediction and the
   persistence baseline.

A missing matching recorded action is a valid archival-resource blocker, not a
reason to choose another separator after outcome access.

## Allowed conclusions

Possible useful results include:

- `revision_frontier_feasible`;
- `revision_generation_or_calibration_blocker`;
- `no_revised_behavioral_divergence`;
- `revised_separator_selected_pre_step2`;
- `no_matching_recorded_step2_action`;
- `revised_prediction_matches_one_candidate`;
- `revised_prediction_outside_frontier`; or
- `recorded_step2_outcome_inconsistent_across_runs`.

Even a successful result remains opened Track A evidence. It would support a
bounded intervention-triggered revision cycle on one public environment, not a
general ontology-discovery theorem, live decision-value claim, sealed transfer,
or Track B claim.

## Stop rule

Do not start revision inference from this planning document alone. First create
and merge a separate freeze containing exact input hashes, prompts, parser,
candidate contract, runtime, isolation, calibration/admission runner, adapter,
OIA invocation, tests, manifests, and allowed verdicts.
