# Project-level scientific reassessment after D169

**Evidence cutoff:** D169, 2026-08-21

**Decision:** stop implementation and materially reformulate the research claim

**Evidence class:** documentation-only synthesis of committed, frozen results;
no learner, evaluator, kernel, family, environment, protocol, or outcome was
created

## 1. Governing objective

The project began with a strong end-to-end objective:

> From unfamiliar evidence and independently authored environments, a system
> should formulate genuinely novel executable ontologies, execute them, detect
> when they are inadequate, revise them, preserve warranted uncertainty, and
> use the resulting knowledge for better and safer decisions.

The accumulated evidence establishes important pieces of that objective, but
not the end-to-end chain. The correct project-level decision is therefore to
preserve the bounded contributions and stop treating another internal bridge as
the default next step.

## 3. The deepest blocker

The deepest blocker is the **independent semantic-authority gap**:

> No demonstrated system takes unfamiliar external evidence, invents the
> semantic mapping and executable structure itself, and then has those
> distinctions independently identified and valued by held-out interventions
> and decisions without evaluator-supplied semantics.

This is deeper than any one solver, representation, interface, or benchmark.

- **Exact synthetic ladder:** N/C results show bounded invention and safe
  reasoning, but stable entities, structured action/effect records, generic
  operators, utilities, and the admissible intervention channel are supplied.
- **BOPTEST:** removes domain names and source equations and therefore supplies
  genuine external behavioral authority. D1 shows formulation and calibration,
  but cross-channel structure hurts prediction, no qualitative effect is
  identifiable, and uncertainty has no independent value. AI1 then finds no
  candidate-separating schedule before action.
- **Symbolic Alchemy:** supplies the strongest independently authored discrete
  rule substrate and a clean anonymous interface. Readiness passes, but no
  learner study opens: AOEL-0.1 cannot construct from empty, AOEL-C1 later fixes
  bounded construction only, and the qualified AOEL-C1 bounds (8 bytes, 16
  actions) do not directly cover the 36-byte/64-action interface.
- **AOEL-C1:** proves representational closure and transactional execution, not
  that histories determine the needed transactions.
- **AOEL-L0/L1:** directly test that missing proposal step and both fail the
  required behavioral/revision frontier gates. Their positive subresults do not
  provide a qualified external learner.
- **PC1/PC1r1:** solve a bounded proof-adjudication problem, not public-history
  semantic construction. PC1r1's strict pass is real, but D169 proves that its
  authority ends before the learner-created compiler/program mapping.
- **AOEL-L2:** cannot be specified honestly without changing that kernel or
  leaving the decisive semantic handoff uncertified.

The project has repeatedly demonstrated that evidence coverage, proposal
coverage, behavioral identification, decision identification, and proof
authority are distinct. The missing result is their external end-to-end
composition.

These paths are mutually exclusive for the next phase. Path 1 deepens internal
proof infrastructure. Path 2 accepts empirical external behavior instead of
proof completeness. Path 3 closes implementation under the current evidence.

## 5. Decision

Choose **Path 3: stop implementation and materially reformulate the claim**.

Path 1 is technically conceivable but has no bounded route to external validity;
even a complete pass would create another internal prerequisite. It is therefore
not the highest-value response to the governing objective.

Path 2 is the scientifically preferable empirical direction in principle, but
it is not presently a credible bounded next phase. The project has no qualified
source-blind learner at the external interface scale, AOEL-C1 does not span the
Symbolic Alchemy byte/action surface, L0/L1 fail the relevant proposal and
revision gates, and the available external interfaces do not currently support
an independently grounded planning endpoint. Combining a new representation,
new learner, external grounding, revision, uncertainty, and planning in one
opened study would be an uninterpretable omnibus experiment; qualifying them
sequentially would recreate the prohibited internal ladder.

The reformulated project claim is:

> Within a finite supplied executable meta-language and a controlled
> observation/intervention interface, exact residual-driven construction can
> invent bounded latent predicates, bindings, observation meanings, and causal
> roles, preserve relevant alternatives, and support safe decisions when
> evidence and proposal coverage are sufficient. Independently authored
> black-box evidence supports anonymous candidate formulation, but this project
> does not establish end-to-end external ontology identification, learner-driven
> revision, semantic grounding, or planning value.

This claim integrates the positive protected synthetic results and the external,
language, learner, active-identification, and proof-handoff negatives without
letting one endpoint substitute for another.

## 6. Hard stopping rule

No additional learner, proof kernel, synthetic family, prompt/model condition,
external runtime study, or protected protocol should be opened in this project.
Documentation, archival reproducibility, and publication-oriented synthesis may
continue, but they must not create new empirical claims.

The project may reopen only if a genuinely new external capability—not another
locally generated family—satisfies all of these conditions before learner work:

1. an independently authored, licensed, resettable executable substrate with at
   least two schema-level development families and one untouched schema-level
   reserve;
2. a learner-visible opaque interface whose fields do not encode source object,
   rule, goal, or action semantics, plus evaluator-private held-out interventions;
3. externally defined outcome and utility/safety signals sufficient to score
   both counterfactual behavior and planning without inventing project-specific
   semantics after outcomes;
4. a scalable constructive learner or representation developed independently of
   the reserve and able to consume the complete public interface without manual
   source templates;
5. a prospective route to test inadequacy, necessary schema revision, uncertainty
   value, and strong model-free/world-model baselines in one bounded opened study;
6. process/provenance isolation that keeps source structure out of proposal,
   ranking, revision, and thresholds; and
7. an outcome-free identifiability audit showing that the permitted interventions
   can distinguish at least one necessary ontology revision from behaviorally
   adequate black-box alternatives.

If any condition is absent, the stop remains in force. Finding such a substrate
or capability would authorize only a new project-level reassessment, not an
automatic experiment.

## 7. Final status

The current project is scientifically complete as a bounded result and boundary
map. The original broad objective remains open, not disproved. Continuing with
the current internal architecture would add machinery faster than it adds
external evidence.
